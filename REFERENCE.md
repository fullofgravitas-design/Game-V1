# REFERENCE — do not narrate this file

## Titles
- Master of the Door: menu. Money Conversion turns objects into yen. Transfer summons the door (locked until he has stood in the cabin). Entry Restriction: only named people pass. Door is indestructible. Destination fixed once crossed.
- Master of the House: owns the Sage's house and the barrier. Monsters cannot cross it. Location unknown until reached.
- Otherworlder: more experience than a native, easier skill learning, no level cap.
- Person Who Visited a Different World For The First Time: easier to invent skills and magic than Pioneer. Growth in an ideal direction. Grants Item Box. Sets BP per level to 100.

## Level-up
Parameters: Magic, Attack, Defense, Agility, Intelligence, Luck. Show HP and MP.
HP = 10 + Defense×2 + level. MP = 3 + Magic×1. Native BP: 10 per level. Otherworlder: 20. First visitor overrides: 100. Unspent BP is free. No level cap.
A kill injects mana and life-force. A jump that moves a stat by about 100+ reconstructs the body: illness and old damage go, appearance follows the new stats. Permanent, carries through the Door.
Any stat over 10000: Human becomes Transcendent Species, level resets to 1, stats kept and slightly raised, later levels slow.
Skills from use, reading, holding a weapon (weapon skills start at 1), or the cheat skills. Garden produce raises a stat once, only in the garden.

## Stat scale
1 sick human. 10 fit laborer. 50 trained soldier. 100 notable knight. 500 elite. 1000 post-Bloody-Ogre baseline. 3000 early musou, kingdom appraisal fails. 10000 evolution. A native at level 40 is not equal to the player at level 40.

## People
Slave or civilian: level 1–5, stats 1–20, one trade or racial skill.
Guard: level 8–15, main stat 40–80, weapon skill 2–3.
Adventurer: level 10–25, main stat 50–150.
Veteran or household knight: level 25–40, main stat 150–400. Needed for an ordinary ogre.
Elite royal knight: level 40–60, main stat 400–800. Still dies to an adult Nest beast.
Court mage: same level, Magic high, body frail.
Human: no racial skill unless a bloodline. Beastkin: same levels, Attack and Agility ×1.2–1.5, one racial skill. Elf: rare, higher Magic and Intelligence, lower Attack, adult often level 30–80. Half-elf between. Dwarf: high Defense and craft, low Agility. Demonfolk: Nest edge, higher base.

## Monsters outside the Nest
Wild dog, boar, wolf: level 3–8, stats 10–40. Goblin: 5–12, stats 20–60. Dire wolf, bear: 10–20, stats 40–120. Ordinary ogre: 15–30, stats 80–250. Mutant: +20–50 levels, one extra skill. Country threat: 80–150, stats 800–3000. Myth: 200+, stats 5000 to unmeasurable.

## Devil's Nest (cabin forest)
No weak side of the fence. Bloody Ogre: level 120–180, Attack/Defense 1500–2500, Agility 800–1200, skills Brutal Strength, Iron Hide, Bite, mild Regeneration, Intimidation. Rank-B stone. One kill takes a level-1 first visitor to about level 100 and ~1000 in each stat before BP.
Hell Slime: level 100–140, high Defense, acid, split, magic resistance. Crystal Deer: 150–200, fire and water. Lesser Nest beast: 80–120, stats 800–1500. Black Fenrir pup: past an elite knight. Adult: level 250+, stats in the thousands. King Mithril Boar: level 300+, Attack past 8000, the evolution kill. Above that: Seven Deadly Sins, two Genesis Dragons. Prison guards are not this list.

## Drops
Finishing blow owns the drop. Items land on the corpse. Appraisal names them and gives the yen price. Luck raises rare chance and can lift a stone one rank. It cannot invent a species drop.
Every magical beast: one stone, ranks F E D C B A S. Prices: F ¥10,000, E ¥50,000, D ¥150,000, C ¥500,000, B ¥1,000,000, A ¥5,000,000, S ¥20,000,000+ and sometimes Not for Sale.
Also one or two materials. Some consumables raise a stat once. Nest hide armor ignores typical attacks and raises one stat while worn. Rare line can bind (Contractor, Not for Sale). Conversion will not take a bound item.
Canon: Bloody Ogre fang ¥500,000, stone B, body and gauntlet raise Attack, waist and legs raise Agility. Hell Slime core ¥500,000, jelly raises Magic and Defense, stone C, rare Black Moon Necklace. Crystal Deer stone S, rare portable bath. Devil Bear hair, meat, blood (flame resistance), stone A, rare Flame Guitar. Nest Goblin General stone A. King Orc stone S and hair brush. Needle Roller steel-piercing needle, stone S.
People drop no stones and no system materials. Loot is carried gear, coin, keys, papers. Predator still takes skills.
